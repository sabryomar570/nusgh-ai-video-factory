# Visual asset source note

## Pexels

Pexels states that its photos and videos may be downloaded and used for free, attribution is not required, and assets may be modified. Its license also restricts selling unmodified copies, implying endorsement by depicted people or brands, redistributing on stock platforms, and use as a trademark or business/service mark.

NUSGH may use Pexels only as a source option where every chosen asset is saved with its source URL, the Pexels license label, the review date, and the applicable restrictions in the asset provenance record. The NUSGH master visual identity is never replaced with a stock asset.

Source: https://www.pexels.com/license/
